# Sindbad Checklists — Staff SOP
*Standard Operating Procedure for opening & closing checklists*

---

## What This App Is

A digital checklist for opening and closing the restaurant. You walk through each task, tick it as you do it, optionally add notes or photos, and submit when finished. The manager sees what was done, when, and by whom.

**Don't have it on your phone yet?** Read the **Install Guide** first.

---

## When to Use It

| Shift | When |
|---|---|
| **Opening** | At the start of your morning shift, BEFORE service begins. Walk through ticking items as you do them. |
| **Closing** | At the end of your evening shift, BEFORE you leave. Walk through ticking items as you do them. |

> ⚠️ Never tick items in advance or in bulk at the end. The point of a checklist is to confirm tasks done in real-time, in the correct order.

---

## Step-by-Step (Every Shift)

### 1. Open the app
Tap the **Sindbad Checklists** icon on your home screen.

### 2. Enter PIN
The PIN is **1981**. Same for all staff.

### 3. Sign in with Google
**First time only on this phone:**
- Tap **Sign in with Google**.
- Pick your own Gmail account.
- The app will remember you for **30 days** — no need to sign in again on this device for a month.

**Already signed in?** You'll skip this step automatically — the app shows your name and email in a pill at the top.

### 4. Enter your name
Type your first name (e.g. `Ali`) in the **Staff Name** field.

### 5. Confirm the date
It's already filled in with today's date. You **cannot** change it — every checklist is locked to today.

### 6. Pick the shift
- Tap **Opening** (gold button) if it's the morning shift.
- Tap **Closing** (teal button) if it's the evening shift.

### 7. Walk through the checklist
As you complete each task in the restaurant, tick it in the app.

**For every task you can:**
- ☑ Tap the **checkbox** when done
- 📝 Add a **note** in the small box if something needs the manager's attention
- 📷 Tap the **camera button** to attach a photo (great for showing dirty equipment, broken items, or low supplies)

### 8. Review & Submit
- When ALL tasks are checked, tap **Review & Submit**.
- A summary appears showing your name, date, shift, and notes/photos count.
- Tap **Submit Checklist**.

> ⚠️ **You CANNOT submit until every task is checked.** The app will warn you and highlight the missing items in red. Go back, finish them, and try again.

### 9. Done
Green success screen appears → tap **Done** → back to PIN screen.

## Rules

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| Tick a task you haven't done | Walk and tick at the same time |
| Share your PIN or Google login | Sign in as yourself |
| Skip the photo on something the manager needs to see | Take photos of broken equipment, low stock, leaks, anything unusual |
| Submit a half-finished checklist | Complete every task first |
| Forget to Sign Out on a shared tablet | Tap **Sign Out** at the end of your shift |

---

## Notes & Photos — When to Use Them

**Add a NOTE when:**
- A task needs explanation (e.g. *"smoothie machine making a clicking sound"*)
- Something is low and needs ordering (e.g. *"ran out of mint by 8pm"*)
- You did the task differently than usual (e.g. *"used backup blender, main one broken"*)

**Add a PHOTO when:**
- Something is broken
- Something is unusually dirty
- You want to show a problem the manager should see
- Anything visual is easier to show than to describe

---

## Sign Out — When to Tap It

**Tap Sign Out:**
- ✅ At end of shift on a **shared tablet**
- ✅ If you're handing the device to another staff member
- ✅ If you used someone else's phone by accident

**Don't bother with Sign Out:**
- ❌ On your own personal phone (the 30-day trust keeps you signed in)

To sign out: on the **Name** screen, tap the **Sign Out** pill at the top.

## What Happens After You Submit

Behind the scenes:
- A row gets added to the **Sindbad Burger Checklists** Google Sheet.
- Your photos are uploaded to the **Sindbad Burger Checklist Photos** Google Drive folder.
- A branded email is sent to **Info@sindbadburger.com** with the full summary.

The manager checks these regularly. If you submit a checklist with notes or photos, expect a follow-up if anything needs action.
