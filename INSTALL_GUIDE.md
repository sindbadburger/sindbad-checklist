# Sindbad Checklists — How to Install on Your Phone

**Why?** So you can open the app from your home screen like a real app — no typing URLs.
It takes 30 seconds.

---

## 📱 On iPhone (Safari)

1. Open **Safari** (must be Safari — not Chrome).
2. Type this address in the bar:
   **https://sindbadburger.github.io/sindbad-checklist**
3. Wait for the page to load — you should see the **Sindbad Checklists** logo (the cartoon Sindbad with a stopwatch).
4. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom (the square with the arrow pointing up).
5. Scroll down in the menu → tap **Add to Home Screen**.
6. Tap **Add** in the top right.
7. ✅ Done — look for the **Sindbad Checklists** icon on your home screen.

---

## 🤖 On Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to: **https://sindbadburger.github.io/sindbad-checklist**
3. Wait for the page to load.
4. Tap the **menu** (the three dots in the top right).
5. Tap **Add to Home screen** → **Add**.
6. ✅ Done — the **Sindbad Checklists** icon appears on your home screen.

---

## ✅ Test It

Tap the new icon on your home screen.
- The app should open **full-screen** (no address bar visible).
- You should see the Sindbad Checklists welcome screen with the stopwatch logo.
- Enter the PIN: **1981**.
- You're in!

---

## ❓ If Something Doesn't Work

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The icon shows a black square with an "S" | Delete it, reload the page, and add to home screen again |
| The app opens in a browser tab, not full-screen | You opened the URL from the browser instead of tapping the icon — go back to your home screen and tap the icon |
| The page won't load | Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data |
| The PIN doesn't work | Ask your manager |
| Anything else | Ask your manager |
